Welcome to the SEAL Lab

We are a security research lab, the SEAL Lab (Systematic sEcurity anAlysis and evaLuation Lab) affiliated with the University of South Florida.

Our research focuses on systematically analyzing and evaluating the security of systems, uncovering vulnerabilities, and building tools and methods to make software more trustworthy.

We are looking for passionate new PhD students, Postdocs, and Master students to join the team. (See Vacancies)
